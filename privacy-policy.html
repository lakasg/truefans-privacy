True Fans Watchlist Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

True Fans Watchlist is a personal TV series and anime tracking application designed to keep a user's watchlist, progress, ratings, notes, and related preferences primarily on the user's device.

1. Information stored on your device

True Fans Watchlist stores information such as your watchlist, watched episodes, watch state, ratings, notes, application settings, notification preferences, and show-unlock information locally on your device.

True Fans Watchlist does not require a user account for normal use.

2. TV and anime information services

True Fans Watchlist connects to third-party services in order to provide television and anime information, search results, episode information, artwork, release dates, and related metadata.

These services may receive information necessary to process a request, such as search terms, show identifiers, episode identifiers, network information, and your IP address.

Services used by the application may include:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB)
  • AniList
  • Jikan / MyAnimeList-related metadata services

Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Advertising

True Fans Watchlist uses rewarded advertisements provided through Google Mobile Ads.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. A user may choose to watch an advertisement in order to unlock additional show additions.

Google Mobile Ads and related Google advertising services may process information including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • App and advertisement interactions
  • Video views, taps, and other advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Fraud-prevention and security information

This information may be used by Google for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Consent and European privacy choices

Where required, True Fans Watchlist uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage consent for advertising-related data processing.

Users in regions where consent or privacy choices are required may be shown a privacy message before advertising requests are made.

Available privacy choices may depend on the user's region and the requirements applicable to Google advertising services.

5. Notifications

True Fans Watchlist can provide local notifications for events such as new episodes, seasons, renewals, and cancellations.

Notification settings and watchlist information are stored locally on the device.

6. Import and export

Users may export and import their True Fans Watchlist data.

Exported files are controlled by the user and may contain personal watchlist information, ratings, notes, progress, and preferences.

7. Data sharing

True Fans Watchlist does not sell personal watchlist information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services only where necessary to provide app functionality, retrieve TV or anime information, provide advertising, obtain consent, perform security functions, or prevent fraud.

8. Data retention

Watchlist information stored locally remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, removed through the application, or removed when application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal requirements.

9. Children's privacy

True Fans Watchlist is not specifically directed to children.

Content information, posters, descriptions, and advertising supplied by third parties may vary depending on the title, region, and service.

10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application, its features, advertising services, or legal requirements change.

The latest version of this policy will be available at the public privacy-policy URL used for True Fans Watchlist.
